## Listing copy

Display name: SureWord (subject to name reservation)

Short description:
Study Scripture with a Bible reader, AI conversations, and your own notes.

Description:
SureWord brings Bible reading and personal study together. Read the King James
Version, ask questions grounded in Scripture, and keep what you learn in notes.

Follow a passage in the Bible reader, explore related Scripture through chat,
and return to saved conversations and studies. Organize notes with folders and
tags, highlight verses, and follow a reading plan. Pick Up Your Cross provides
a daily verse and question for reflection.

The Windows app opens SureWord in its own window and includes a Start-menu
entry. Sign in to access your account. An internet connection is required for
AI conversations and synced account features. Available AI models and optional
features depend on your account settings.
