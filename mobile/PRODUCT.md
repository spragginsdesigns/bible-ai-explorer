# SureWord Android

## Platform

android

## Product Purpose

SureWord helps people study the Bible, ask Scripture questions, and keep study notes. Android is the primary client. Preserve its chat API, account boundaries, consent, history, attachments, and Bible and Notes destinations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The signed-in chat home presents questions from the user's study and accepts a custom question. Preserve all suggestions in server order and keep the composer docked when the welcome becomes a conversation. Longer questions, missing labels, large text, the keyboard, light appearance, and reduced motion must remain usable.

## Brand Commitments

Austin approved the friendly folded-page companion and four-question grid with charcoal, ivory, and gold colors on 2026-10-12. The SureWord wordmark and open-Bible day-star mark remain recognizable. He requested a prayerful upward-reaching thinking pose and a two-arm praising pose while answering.
