A stale yt-dlp was the entire download problem

Fixed

Whisper reproduces the KJV accurately enough to quote

Confirmed

The scripture reading came through effectively verbatim, including archaic forms that a weaker model mangles. This is what makes honest verse linking possible: the spoken text can be matched against KjvVerse rather than trusted on its own.

A just ended stream cannot be pulled at all

Scheduling constraint

Today's service is still a live DVR manifest, not a finished VOD. The normal clients answer This live event has ended and the fallback client downloads fragment by fragment at near realtime. A cron firing at service end would fail every week.

Evidence

Two hours after the stream ended, cn_KsMbj7Ec reached 1.5 MB in roughly ten minutes before stalling. The job needs to wait, then retry until the VOD is processed, rather than pull on a fixed clock.

The sermon is a third of the way into the file

Design input

The recording opens with five minutes of silence, then announcements about an association meeting and the Harvest Festival. The preaching text is not announced until 31:07. Segmentation is a required step, not a nicety, or the study will be built partly out of a sign up sheet reminder.

Ingestion cannot run on Vercel

Architecture

Three independent reasons: functions cap at 300 seconds, there is no GPU, and YouTube throttles datacenter IPs. The download and transcription belong on this PC, which then posts finished text to the SureWord API. Everything after transcription is ordinary server work.

The honesty rule that has to be enforced in the prompt

Rule

Every claim about what the pastor said anchors to a timestamped quote from the transcript, and SureWord's own teaching is rendered in a visually separate block. The study may never put words in his mouth. Quoted scripture is re rendered from KjvVerse, never from the transcript, so a mis heard word cannot become a misquoted verse.

Shape of a section 
{ heading, startMs, youtubeUrlWithTimestamp,
  pastorQuote,        // verbatim from transcript
  passage,            // verified against KjvVerse
  explanation,        // SureWord's teaching, marked as such
  crossRefs[], reflectionQuestion, imageUrl }

Next steps

  1. Generate one full guided study from the transcript already on disk. Content quality is now the only unproven risk, and it is the risk that decides whether this ships. No database work required to find out.
  2. Wrap the proven commands into scripts/sermon/ingest.mjs. Discover via RSS, self update yt-dlp, download, transcribe, retry while the VOD is still processing. Output markdown and images to disk for review.
  3. Add SermonStudy and the API, then the Android screen. Android leads per the parity rule, web follows in the same cycle, and docs/PARITY.md gets a row.
  4. Schedule it on this PC. Task Scheduler on Sunday afternoon and Wednesday night, with the wait and retry built in, then a push notification when the study lands.
  5. Only then generalize beyond FMBC. UserChurch already stores the church, so attaching a channel per user is the natural extension once the FMBC case is good.
